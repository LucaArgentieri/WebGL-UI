details

Native <details> elements. The content appears with a WebGL dissolve and the height animates in sync; open, keyboard and the accessibility tree stay native.

What stays native?

Everything behavioral: Enter and Space on the summary, the open attribute and the expanded state announced by screen readers.

The canvas is aria-hidden, ignores the pointer and only exists during the animation.

What does the shader do?

A fragment shader covers the content with the surface color and dissolves it with fractal noise, top to bottom, with a glowing edge along the front.

Standard uniforms: uTime, uProgress, uResolution, uPointer.

What if WebGL is missing?

The click is not intercepted and the browser opens and closes the details as usual. Same with prefers-reduced-motion: reduce and with a lost WebGL context.
